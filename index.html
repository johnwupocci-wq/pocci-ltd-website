About LUXCEL — Turnkey Storage Packaging & Testing Services

LUXCEL delivers one-stop storage OSAT solutions worldwide: greenfield packaging & testing factory build-out, new and refurbished OSAT equipment, production operation management, NAND/DRAM wafer sourcing, storage controller tape-out facilitation, and OEM/ODM manufacturing. Product coverage includes UDP, TF (microSD), SSD (M.2 / SATA / NVMe), SD-NAND, DDR3/4/5, LPDDR3/4/5 and eMMC, with export certification support (CE / FCC / RoHS).
