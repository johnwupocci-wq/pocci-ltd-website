行情解读：今天的关键词是「背离」——上游 NAND 现货与渠道成品同步松动，DRAM 原装颗粒却仍钉在 60 美元上方；资本市场因英伟达 CEO 回归而大涨，终端销量数据却在继续恶化。

信号一：NAND 的拐点从「预期」变成「数据」。NANDWatcher／DRAMeXchange 11-24 口径显示 512Gb TLC 晶圆 US$14.24，日盘止跌但 7 日仍微跌 0.2%；TrendForce 经快科技 11-24 披露的口径为 US$14.2，较 10 月中旬下跌 20%、较 8 月高点回撤约 32%。合约端出现罕见分歧——TrendForce 11-19 仍看 Q4 整体 +8%～13%，11-24 转述口径则把消费级 Q4 收窄到 0%～5%，2027 Q1 持平至 -5%，美银更看 -5%～10%。方向比数字更重要：NAND 已从「涨多少」切换到「什么时候跌」。

信号二：渠道是本周最先松动的环节。闪德 11-24 报价全面刷新：DDR5 16G/6000 C36 ¥1,620（-2.41%）、32G/5600 ¥3,395（-1.59%），DDR4 16G/3200 ¥650（-1.07%），NV3 1TB ¥700（-3.05%），D5 NB 16G ¥1,510（-0.66%）。这是两周横盘后的首次全系列回落，配合【四十七周】周评中 OEM SSD 全容量下跌 4%～10%、TF 卡跌 7%～17%、UDP 跌 5%～9%，说明议价权已经从卖方转到买方。

信号三：DRAM 的高位是结构性的，不是全面的。DDR5 16Gb 60.833 美元再涨 0.22%，但 DDR4 16Gb／8Gb 与白片继续阴跌；IDC 预计 2027 年 HBM4 价格最高涨 170%、HBM 占晶圆投入由 23% 升至 30%，直接挤掉约 7.9% 的 PC DRAM 位元供应。紧的是 AI 用内存，松的是消费级资源，两者不能用同一句「内存还在涨」概括。

信号四：供给在扩，需求在缩。TrendForce 11-24 报道三星西安 X2 厂将把 V9 eSSD 月产能从 1 万片提到 5 万片，2027 年 11 月完成；另一边长江存储第三座 10 万片/月工厂落地、2027 年产能 +24.2% 并拟进入 DRAM。需求侧更刺眼：三星前三季度手机内存采购均价同比涨 263%，整机均价只涨 13%，于是 Galaxy A 砍产 20%、2027 年干脆停发 Galaxy Watch。当终端只能靠降配和砍单消化成本，价格的顶部就已经不远。

给采购客户的行动建议：① NAND／SSD／TF 卡按住库存、分批下单——现货较 8 月高点已回撤约 32%，Q1 合约转跌预期明确，不必在此时囤半年以上用量；② 渠道价刚转跌，可趁 11 月这轮回调重谈季度价，把 1TB SSD、DDR5 高频条作为优先议价品类；③ DDR5 原装颗粒不要赌大跌——60 美元关口九连守，HBM 挤占产能是实打实的，刚需按 4～6 周滚动补货；④ DDR4 成熟制程仍可再等，但 D2／D3 转单与封测瓶颈并存，长交期料号请提前锁产能而非只锁价格；⑤ 长约要写明「量价分离」——最低采购量、交付兑现率、价格重谈窗口与提前退出条款，避免签完被追加成本；⑥ 终端设计尽快准备降配方案（三星、特斯拉均已砍规格），避免量产前被迫改板；⑦ 别把股价当货价——美光单日 +19%、海力士 +20% 是情绪修复，现货与渠道仍在下行，按公开现货盘谈价更稳。

Market read: the keyword today is divergence — upstream NAND spot and channel finished goods are softening together, while branded DRAM dies remain pinned above US$60; equities rallied on the NVIDIA CEO news while end-market sales data keep deteriorating.

Signal 1: NAND's turning point has moved from expectation to data. NANDWatcher / DRAMeXchange shows 512Gb TLC at US$14.24 on 11-24: the daily session stabilized but the 7-day trend is still -0.2%. The TrendForce figure cited by Fast Technology on 11-24 is US$14.2, down 20% since mid-October and about 32% from the August high. Contract views differ — TrendForce's 11-19 forecast still had Q4 overall at +8% to +13%, while the 11-24 account narrows consumer Q4 to 0%-5% with 2027 Q1 flat to -5%, and BofA sees -5% to -10%. Direction matters more than the exact number: NAND has shifted from "how much is it rising" to "when will it fall."

Signal 2: the channel is the first link to loosen. Shande's 11-24 quotes are broadly lower: DDR5 16G/6000 C36 ¥1,620 (-2.41%), 32G/5600 ¥3,395 (-1.59%), DDR4 16G/3200 ¥650 (-1.07%), NV3 1TB ¥700 (-3.05%) and D5 NB 16G ¥1,510 (-0.66%). This is the first broad decline after two weeks of flat pricing, reinforced by the Week 47 review showing OEM SSD prices down 4%-10% across capacities, TF cards down 7%-17% and UDP down 5%-9%. Bargaining power has shifted from sellers to buyers.

Signal 3: DRAM's high level is structural, not universal. DDR5 16Gb rose 0.22% to US$60.833, but DDR4 16Gb/8Gb and eTT dies keep falling. IDC expects HBM4 prices up as much as 170% in 2027 and HBM's share of wafer input to rise from 23% to 30%, directly removing about 7.9% of PC DRAM bit supply. What is tight is AI memory, not memory in general.

Signal 4: supply is expanding while demand is shrinking. TrendForce reported on 11-24 that Samsung will raise V9 eSSD wafer output at Xi'an X2 from 10k to 50k per month by Nov 2027; meanwhile YMTC's third 100k-wafer/month fab is in place and 2027 capacity is up 24.2%, with a DRAM entry planned. Demand-side numbers are starker: Samsung's mobile memory purchase price is up 263% year over year while handset ASP rose only 13%, leading to a 20% Galaxy A output cut and no Galaxy Watch in 2027. When end customers can only absorb costs through lower specs and lower volume, the price top is close.

Advice for procurement teams: ① Keep NAND / SSD / TF card inventory lean and buy in batches — spot is about 32% below the August high and Q1 contract declines are broadly expected, so avoid stocking six months of demand. ② Use this November correction to renegotiate quarterly prices, prioritizing 1TB SSDs and high-speed DDR5 modules. ③ Do not bet on a sharp DDR5 die collapse — the US$60 level has held for nine sessions and HBM crowd-out is real; cover rigid demand on a 4-6 week rolling basis. ④ DDR4 can wait, but lock capacity rather than just price for long-lead parts, given D2/D3 order transfers and OSAT bottlenecks. ⑤ Separate volume from price in contracts, with minimum purchase quantities, delivery fulfilment rates, price-reopen windows and early-exit terms. ⑥ Prepare spec-down alternatives in designs now (Samsung and Tesla have already cut memory specs) to avoid last-minute board changes. ⑦ Do not treat equity moves as goods prices — Micron +19% and SK hynix +20% reflect sentiment repair; negotiate against public spot quotes instead.
