行情解读：今天的关键词是「分化」——高盛、美银与 TrendForce 三方数据首次同时确认：DRAM 紧、NAND 松，两条周期正式分道扬镳。

信号一：DRAM 与 NAND 的周期正式分开。高盛预计 2027 年全球 DRAM 将出现十五年以上最严重的供应短缺，SK 海力士／三星传统 DRAM 均价 2027 年分别上涨 32%／31%；但美银数据显示 NAND 现货自 10 月中下旬以来已跌超 20%，2027 Q1 通用 IT 领域 NAND 还要再跌 5%～10%。TrendForce 11-25 官方 Insights 给出实锤：主流 DDR4（1Gx8 3200）周均价从 US$44.03 跌到 US$43.32，周跌 1.61%；512Gb TLC 晶圆周跌 3.18% 至 US$13.786，卖方为去库存主动软化报价，买方仅按实际需求小量补货。「内存还在涨」这句话已经不成立，必须分开看 DRAM 和 NAND。

信号二：品牌 DDR5 结束上涨， DDR4／白片／DDR3 全面阴跌。TrendForce 11-25 18:10 收盘盘 DDR5 16Gb 微跌 0.11% 至 US$60.767，仍守 60 美元关口；DDR4 16Gb -0.64%、8Gb -0.25%、DDR3 4Gb -0.26%，白片跌幅更大。报告明确指出：消费电子需求疲软，买方预期价格下跌、优先去库存风险，现货询价多只为探市而非真实补货。

信号三：渠道延续跌势，议价权仍在买方。闪德 11-25 渠道价：DDR4 16G/3200 ¥640（较前一日 ¥650 再跌，周 -1.54%）、DDR5 16G/6000 C36 ¥1,620（较上周 ¥1,670 跌约 3%）、D5 NB 32G ¥3,520（-0.85%）、NV3 1TB ¥700（较上周 ¥742 跌约 5.7%）。配合 CFM 当日「行业及渠道 SSD 报价下调」与新增 2Tb QLC 晶圆报价，消费级存储供给压力仍在累积。

信号四：技术格局生变。TrendForce 11-25 报道，SK 海力士继三星之后也与长江存储达成 Xtacking 交叉授权。在 400 层以上 NAND 转向晶圆键合的竞赛中，长江存储 2017 年至 2024 年 1 月公开 119 件混合键合相关专利，三星 83 件、SK 海力士仅 11 件——韩厂要推进 V10 BV（400+ 层）与 4D NAND（375 层）绕不开中国专利。这对中长期 NAND 供给格局的影响，比短期现货涨跌更值得关注。

给采购客户的行动建议：① NAND／SSD／TF 卡按住库存、分批下单——现货自 10 月中已跌超 20%，2027 Q1 转跌预期明确，不必囤半年以上用量；② DDR4 与白片可以再等——周跌 1.61%、消费级需求疲软，但 D2／D3 转单与封测瓶颈并存，长交期料号提前锁产能；③ DDR5 原装不要赌大跌——高盛看 2027 十五年最严重短缺，60 美元关口未破，刚需按 4～6 周滚动补货；④ 趁渠道回调重谈季度价，1TB SSD、DDR4 16G、DDR5 高频条作为优先议价品类；⑤ 长约写明「量价分离」——最低采购量、交付兑现率、价格重谈窗口与提前退出条款，避免签完被追加成本；⑥ 关注终端降配信号（2027 手机出货目标多数下调、RTX 60 或延期），提前准备替代方案；⑦ 别把股价当货价——AI 芯片交期拉长与存储现货走弱并存，按公开现货盘谈价更稳。

Market read: the keyword today is divergence — for the first time, data from Goldman Sachs, BofA and TrendForce jointly confirm it: DRAM is tight, NAND is loose, and the two cycles have formally split.

Signal 1: the DRAM and NAND cycles have separated. Goldman Sachs expects the most severe DRAM shortage in more than 15 years in 2027, with conventional DRAM average prices at SK hynix and Samsung up 32% and 31% respectively. BofA data, however, shows NAND spot prices already down over 20% since mid-to-late October, with general IT NAND set to fall another 5%-10% in 2027 Q1. TrendForce's official 11-25 Insights provides confirmation: the mainstream DDR4 (1Gx8 3200) weekly average fell from US$44.03 to US$43.32, down 1.61% week-on-week, while 512Gb TLC wafers fell 3.18% to US$13.786 as sellers softened quotes to clear inventory and buyers replenished only in small lots. "Memory prices are still rising" is no longer accurate — DRAM and NAND must be assessed separately.

Signal 2: branded DDR5 stops rising while DDR4 / eTT / DDR3 all decline. In TrendForce's 11-25 18:10 close, DDR5 16Gb slipped 0.11% to US$60.767, still holding the US$60 mark; DDR4 16Gb fell 0.64%, 8Gb 0.25% and DDR3 4Gb 0.26%, with eTT parts down more. The report notes that weak consumer-electronics demand has buyers expecting further declines and prioritizing inventory-risk reduction, with most spot inquiries being price checks rather than genuine restocking.

Signal 3: channel prices keep falling and bargaining power remains with buyers. Shande's 11-25 quotes: DDR4 16G/3200 ¥640 (down again from ¥650 the prior day, -1.54% weekly), DDR5 16G/6000 C36 ¥1,620 (about 3% below ¥1,670 last week), D5 NB 32G ¥3,520 (-0.85%) and NV3 1TB ¥700 (about 5.7% below ¥742 last week). Together with CFM's same-day note that industry and channel SSD quotes were cut and new 2Tb QLC wafer quotations appeared, supply pressure in consumer storage continues to build.

Signal 4: the technology landscape is shifting. TrendForce reported on 11-25 that SK hynix has joined Samsung in a cross-licensing deal for YMTC's Xtacking technology. As NAND above 400 layers moves to wafer-to-wafer bonding, YMTC published 119 hybrid-bonding patents between 2017 and January 2024, versus 83 for Samsung and just 11 for SK hynix — Korean makers cannot advance their V10 BV (400+ layer) and 4D NAND (375-layer) roadmaps without navigating Chinese patents. For medium- to long-term NAND supply, this matters more than short-term spot swings.

Action notes for procurement teams: ① Keep NAND / SSD / TF-card inventory low and order in batches — spot prices are already down over 20% since mid-October and 2027 Q1 declines are widely expected, so avoid stockpiling six months or more; ② DDR4 and eTT parts can wait — down 1.61% weekly amid soft consumer demand, though D2/D3 order shifts and backend bottlenecks persist, so lock capacity rather than just price for long-lead parts; ③ Do not bet on a sharp DDR5 drop — Goldman sees the worst DRAM shortage in 15 years in 2027 and the US$60 level has held, so cover rigid demand on a rolling 4-6 week basis; ④ Renegotiate quarterly terms during this channel pullback, prioritizing 1TB SSDs, DDR4 16G and high-frequency DDR5 modules; ⑤ Write volume-price separation into contracts — minimum purchase volume, delivery fulfilment, price-reopening windows and early-exit terms, to avoid post-signature cost additions; ⑥ Watch for end-market spec cuts (most 2027 handset shipment targets revised down, RTX 60 may slip) and prepare alternatives early; ⑦ Do not read goods prices from equities — longer AI chip lead times coexist with weakening memory spot prices, so negotiate against published spot boards.
