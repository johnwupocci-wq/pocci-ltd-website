行情解读：今天的主线是「涨价周期见顶信号密集出现」——不是现货一日大跌，而是预测、终端、渠道三层同时转向。

信号一：TrendForce 亲手下修 2027，周期路径被改写。TrendForce 11-25 Market View 将 2027 年 DRAM 产值下修至 1.15 万亿美元（同比 +69%）、NAND 下修至 4,193 亿美元（同比 +29%）。DRAM 仍偏紧，但合约涨幅逐季收敛、下半年可能持平；NAND 在供给扩张与中系 CSP 削减企业级 SSD 采购的双重压力下进入下行循环，合约价自 Q1 起转跌。11-27 盘前复盘引述其最新季度预期：2027 Q1～Q4 DRAM 合约价环比由 10%/7%/5%/3% 下调至 6%/3%/0%/0%——涨价周期大概率在 2027 年年中见顶。

信号二：涨价终于反噬终端，手机厂集体砍单。闪德 11-26 报道，全球主流手机厂下调 2027 出货目标：小米由 1.2 亿部降至 9,000 万部（-25%）、vivo 由 8,500 万降至 6,000 万（-29%）、OPPO -14%、华为压到 5,000 万部以下（-16% 以上）、苹果 -5.6%、三星 -9.5%。原因是产能向服务器／HBM 倾斜后，手机端 DRAM 同时遭遇缺货与涨价，利润空间被压缩，厂商主动控量、减少备货。这是需求破坏从「传闻」变成「出货计划」的硬证据。

信号三：现货横盘、渠道全跌，价差由渠道承担。TrendForce 11-26 18:10 收盘盘：DDR5 16Gb US$60.767 连续两日持平，DDR4 16Gb -0.32%、8Gb -0.21%、DDR3 -0.20%，白片跌幅更大。但闪德 11-26 渠道价几乎全线下调：DDR5 16G/5600 ¥1,477（-2.83%）、DDR4 16G ¥630（-1.56%）、D5 NB 16G ¥1,480（-1.99%）、NV3 1TB ¥693（较上周 ¥742 实跌约 6.6%）。现货不跌、成品跌，说明模组与渠道在自行消化库存，而不是上游主动让利。

信号四：NAND 逼近关键支撑，DRAM 库存与减产博弈。512Gb TLC 现货 US$13.786 已逼近 US$13.79 支撑位，另一口径（11-26 收盘）报 US$15.00 持平，机构样本差异较大；合约端 Q4 涨幅已从 15~20% 砍到 8~13%。同时市场传出通路端库存回到约 16 个月（常态 12 个月）、原厂准备减产备案但尚未执行——库存是可核事实，减产仍只是预案，不宜据此抢反弹。

给采购客户的行动建议：① NAND／SSD／TF 卡继续按单采购、勿囤长库存——合约转跌与渠道价下行已同步，1TB SSD 可重点议价；② DDR4 与白片还有下行空间但别赌崩盘——现货仍高于 10 月合约约 57%，减产备案未落地，长交期料号提前锁产能；③ DDR5 原装按 4～6 周滚动补货——60 美元关口两日未破、三星 2027 产能已售罄，刚需不宜空仓等大跌；④ 趁渠道普跌重谈季度价，DDR5 16G/5600、DDR4 16G、D5 NB 32G、NV3 1TB 列为优先议价品类；⑤ 长约坚持量价分离与价格重谈窗口，约定最低采购量、交付兑现率与退出条款，避免锁量后被追加成本；⑥ 为终端降配做准备（华硕入门机 16GB→8GB、手机厂集体下调出货），提前沟通替代料号与容量档位；⑦ 把股价与货价分开看——存储股普跌反映的是 2027 预期下修，实际采购仍按公开现货盘与渠道报价谈。

Market read: Today's theme is "peak-signals are stacking up" — not a one-day spot collapse, but a simultaneous turn in forecasts, end demand and channel pricing.

Signal 1: TrendForce itself cuts 2027, rewriting the path. Its 11-25 Market View lowers the 2027 DRAM revenue forecast to US$1.15 tn (+69% YoY) and NAND to US$419.3 bn (+29% YoY). DRAM stays undersupplied, but quarterly contract gains narrow and could flatten in H2; NAND enters a downcycle as supply expands and Chinese CSPs trim enterprise SSD orders, with contract prices turning down from Q1. A 11-27 pre-market recap cites a further cut to 2027 DRAM contract QoQ forecasts: from 10%/7%/5%/3% to 6%/3%/0%/0% for Q1-Q4 — the price peak likely lands around mid-2027.

Signal 2: Higher prices finally hit end demand; smartphone makers cut orders. Shande reported on 11-26 that leading smartphone vendors are lowering 2027 shipment targets: Xiaomi from 120m to 90m (-25%), vivo from 85m to 60m (-29%), OPPO -14%, Huawei below 50m (-16%+), Apple -5.6% and Samsung -9.5%. With capacity shifted to servers and HBM, handset makers face both shortage and higher costs, compressing margins and prompting active inventory control. Demand destruction has moved from rumour to shipment plan.

Signal 3: Spot flat, channel broadly down; the channel absorbs the gap. TrendForce's 11-26 18:10 close: DDR5 16Gb US$60.767 flat for a second session, DDR4 16Gb -0.32%, 8Gb -0.21%, DDR3 -0.20%, with white-label die falling faster. Shande's 11-26 channel quotes, however, fell almost across the board: DDR5 16G/5600 CNY 1,477 (-2.83%), DDR4 16G CNY 630 (-1.56%), D5 NB 16G CNY 1,480 (-1.99%), NV3 1TB CNY 693 (about -6.6% from CNY 742 a week earlier). Spot holding while finished goods fall means module makers and distributors are working off inventory, not that upstream is conceding price.

Signal 4: NAND nears key support; DRAM inventory versus output-cut talk. 512Gb TLC spot at US$13.786 is close to the US$13.79 support level, while an alternative 11-26 close series shows US$15.00 flat — institutional samples differ materially. Contract Q4 gains have been cut from 15-20% to 8-13%. Meanwhile, channel inventory is said to have returned to about 16 months (normal about 12), and makers are preparing, but have not executed, output cuts. Inventory is a measurable fact; production cuts are still only a plan — not a reason to chase a rebound.

Action notes for buyers: ① Keep NAND/SSD/TF-card buying to actual demand; avoid long stockpiles — contracts are turning down and channel prices are falling, with 1TB SSD a priority for price talks; ② DDR4 and white-label die may fall further, but a collapse is not the base case — spot is still about 57% above the October contract price and cuts are not yet executed, so lock capacity for long-lead parts; ③ Roll DDR5 branded die on a 4-6 week basis — the US$60 level has held for two sessions and Samsung's 2027 capacity is sold out; do not run down to zero waiting for a crash; ④ Renegotiate quarterly prices into the channel decline, starting with DDR5 16G/5600, DDR4 16G, D5 NB 32G and NV3 1TB; ⑤ Keep volume and price separate in LTAs, with minimum volumes, delivery fulfilment, price-review windows and exit clauses; ⑥ Prepare for downstream downgrades (Asus entry models 16GB to 8GB, broad handset target cuts) by agreeing alternative parts and capacity tiers early; ⑦ Separate equity prices from physical prices — the memory selloff prices in a 2027 downgrade, while purchasing should still be negotiated against public spot and channel quotes.
